# Blackonix design references

Researched 19 October 2026. Sources were retrieved live through Firecrawl. References guide an original implementation; no template code or proprietary photography was copied.

## Recommended direction

Use charcoal, warm off-white, and one burnt-orange accent. Give the headline room, show one real jobsite photograph, and make the free package test the strongest action. Use square corners, fine rules, numbered steps, and typography that feels like a clear set of plans. Keep body text large enough for a phone in daylight.

The useful image is a jobsite, not an abstract technology illustration. Pair it with a small, clearly labelled example of a document becoming a structured draft. Explain the human review step. The owner requested filled testimonial examples. Display them as Sample testimonials with an explicit note that they are illustrative examples, not client endorsements. Replace them with real approved quotes when available.

## Templates worth studying

1. **[Refit by JJ Gerrish](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/templates/refit/)**. [Live demo](https://refit.framer.website/). The official listing offers a free remix. Its preview uses a charcoal split hero, generous typography, one large photograph, and a direct contact action. Borrow the hierarchy and restrained contrast. Replace residential interiors with construction work. Do not copy the sample testimonial or rating. A free Framer remix does not establish permission to export and reuse its source code on another platform.

2. **[Construktion X by BRIX Templates](https://webflow.com/templates/html/construktion-x-construction-website-template)**. [Live demo](https://construktiontemplate.webflow.io/). The official preview shows monochrome type, alternating dark and light sections, full-height photography, and clear service sections. Borrow its substantial typography and editorial spacing. Remove sample customer logos and numerical proof. The official listing requires purchase and specifies a single-use license for one end product. No purchase was made. Exact checkout price was not established.

3. **[Engineering by wcopilot](https://webflow.com/templates/html/engineering-website-template)**. [Live demo](https://engineering-wcopilot.webflow.io/). The official listing includes separate work-process, services, FAQ, and contact layouts, with responsive support down to 360px. Use that information order as a reference for explaining a custom service. Blackonix needs only one page with four workflows and three steps. The listing requires purchase and includes a single-use license. No purchase was made. Exact checkout price was not established.

## Build decisions

- Build original components for Vercel instead of buying a template or introducing another hosting subscription.
- Use one optimized hero image and avoid video, carousels, and decorative animation.
- Keep navigation to workflows, process, about, and contact.
- Use “Send one old bid package” as the primary action.
- Ask for contact details first. Provide a private document handoff after contact unless a secure upload service is configured and tested.
- Describe confidentiality plainly. Do not imply certifications, guaranteed security, or measured savings.
