# Blackonix visual system

## Direction
Industrial field manual, translated into a sharp contractor-facing website. Large condensed lettering, decisive rectangular orange controls, warm off-white document surfaces, dark steel backgrounds and a real construction photograph. Restrained palette follows the user-pinned brief. A large photo brings the physical work into view; a labeled bid specimen makes the office-work offer tangible.

## Tokens
- Steel: #202522, deep steel: #171c19, paper: #f3f1e9, ink: #242a26.
- Accent: #ff783b; use dark ink on orange. Secondary ink #5b625c and secondary light text #bfc6bf.
- Display: locally hosted Barlow Condensed Bold, uppercase, tight line-height, maximum 6rem. Body: system sans-serif, generous 1.6 line-height.
- Max content width 1280px. Desktop side gutters 64px, tablet 32px, phone 22px.
- Rectangular buttons and input controls, no decorative card chrome. Dividers separate real rows. Orange not used for small text on white.

## Composition
Asymmetric hero: left typography, copy and action; right image continues to the edge of its frame with a dark field-notes caption. A horizontal trust line follows. Light workflow section is a wide typographic list paired with a document specimen. The dark numbered process has exactly three sequential steps. The founder section was removed at the owner’s request. The form ends the page on a warm paper surface.

## Behavior
One authored reveal on the illustrative document as it enters view, using a short clipping transition. Content remains visible without scripting. Reduced motion disables animated scrolling and the reveal. Hover colors and focus outlines remain legible. Form uses native required/email validation, then creates a mailto draft with clear state and fallback address.

## Responsive
Hero image stacks below the action on phones. Main heading scales to viewport width. Workflow specimen, process and about sections collapse to one column. Form fields use one column below 600px. All controls have a minimum 44px target. No sticky banners or navigation overlays obscure content.
