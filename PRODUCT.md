## Platform

## Users
Owners, presidents, operations leaders and chief estimators at Greater Houston contractors with roughly 50 to 200 employees. Their teams work across email, spreadsheets, PDFs and field notes. They often visit on a phone, between jobs or in a truck.

## Product Purpose
Make Blackonix a credible local business after a personal outreach email. Invite one old bid package for a free, concrete test of removing repetitive office work. First business goals: free tests, a paying customer and three recurring support clients.

## Positioning
Custom software built around each contractor's existing documents and process. The estimator retains pricing and review. Owner Solomon Wakhungu is a senior software engineer based in Houston/Cypress, Texas, operating Blackonix LLC alongside full-time work.

## Operating Context
Bids, specs, takeoffs, submittals, permits, safety documents, daily reports, invoices and change orders. Trades: electrical; mechanical, HVAC and plumbing; fire, low voltage and controls; civil and underground utilities; industrial services; building trades and general contractors.

No guaranteed savings, invented results, testimonials or customer names. Experimental pricing stays unpublished. Business budget under $500, 5 to 10 hours weekly, no debt.
